The academic year's schedule

The academic year begins exceptionally early due to the planned moving of the main UMPRUM building to Kasárna Karlín in January 2026. It starts 15th September 2025 and finishes 1st July 2026. In the winter semester 2025, all classes, seminars and workshops start 15th September and finish 27th November 2024; the spring semester 2026 begins 16th February and ends 7th May 2026.

The time from 28th November to 11th December and from 8th May to 28th May is a period for preparation of the end-of-semester work, which first-year students (and second-year architecture students) must install by 12th December (winter semester) and 29th May (spring semester).

There’s no need to come before 15th September or do anything special like signing up for classes, picking up your student card or paying for the semester. Everything can be done the first week of October.
HOLIDAYS!!

If you want to go for longer holidays during the academic year, the best time is between the end of December and mid-February (remember that you must take all the exams mandatory for your discipline by the beginning of the following semester). The academic year officially finishes the end of June; however, all first-year students can leave – if they have taken all their exams – after the de-installation of their end-of-semester work.
