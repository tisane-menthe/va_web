VA Curricula

Each semester in the two-year program (three-year for Architecture students), you must gain at least 30 credits. 1 UMPRUM credit represents 1 ECTS credit (ECTS stands for European Credit Transfer System). To be able to enrol in the second year of your studies, you must have 60 credits for the whole first year (2 semesters x 30 credits) by mid-September.

It’s possible to miss only 6 credits for 2 theoretical lectures mandatory for your discipline and transfer these to the following semester; if you don’t have enough credits by mid-September, you must repeat the whole academic year again (and pay a reduced tuition again). The total number of theoretical lectures you must attend during the two years of your studies is 8 lectures mandatory for your discipline (10 for architecture students.

To be able to graduate from the master’s degree program, you must gain 120 credits (4 semesters x 30 credits) by the day of the graduation ceremony (180 credits in three-year architecture study program). For more details, see the Study Plans section on the UMPRUM intranet.

First-year students (and second-year architecture)

Both in the winter and spring semesters, i.e. from 15th September to 27th November, and from 16th February to 7th May, you must attend and earn 60 credits for:
  • group and individual consultations in your studio (e.g. Type Design and Typography, Fine Arts III, etc.)
  • VA meetings with the VA tutor
  • four theoretical lectures mandatory for your discipline (to have 8 x 3 credits by mid-September at least one elective seminar/workshop
  • presentation (and installation) of your end-of-semester project called klauzura
By 14th September 2026, you must earn 60 credits (30 credits for each semester) to be able to sit the state exam and enrol in the second year of your master’s studies. (to enrol, it’s possible to miss six credits for two theoretical lectures and transfer these lectures to the following year).
IMPORTANT!!

In mid-September (one day between 14th and 16th September), you will have to sit the state exam. The state exam is an in-person exam about the history and theory of art and visual culture in general. The questions / topics you will be asked to present are based on the topics that are covered in the theoretical lectures you attended in the first year of your studies. They can be found on the intranet.

This is what the state examination usually looks like: you will be asked to come in the examination room, draw two numbers, i.e. two topics, and you will then have 20 minutes for preparation, and 20 minutes for the presentation of the two topics/ questions before a jury consisting of at least 3 experts on the particular fields (fine arts, graphics, applied arts and design, and architecture). The first question will be on the theory of art in general, and the second one will concern the history of the discipline you are gaining your MFA in (Fine Arts, Design, Applied Arts, Architecture, Graphics). Note: You have three attempts at passing the final state exam, however, if you happen to fail for the third time, your studies will have to be terminated.

Diploma students (i.e. second-year students + third-year architecture students)

In the winter semester 2025, i.e. from 15th September to 27th November, you must regularly attend and earn at least 28 credits for:
  • consultations in your studio (these meetings are called Diploma Seminar 1 – Diploma Project in Progress)
  • more theoretical lectures (mandatory for your field if necessary) to earn 80 credits by January 2026
  • Creative Research seminar
  • individual consultations with your thesis consultant (these are called Diploma Seminar 2 – Diploma Thesis in Progress). The consultant should guide you through the process of writing your thesis, which is a crucial part of your diploma project. The thesis should explain or thematically accompany your diploma project and must around 20 pages long (at least 1800 characters × 20 in total) The deadline for submitting the first part of the thesis (at least 10 pages of text) to the consultant of your project is mid-January. For more details how to write the thesis, see the intranet.
IMPORTANT!!

By mid-October, you will have to submit your diploma work topic (i.e. a filled-in diploma form) to the VA Coordinator and the name of your consultant, i.e. a person you consult your thesis with. The form and the list of recommended consultants you will have to email to can be found on the UMPRUM intranet. The form must be signed by both the consultant and the head of your studio and the consultant you addressed to prove that they approve of the topic you are planning on dealing with for one year in your diploma project.
IMPORTANT!! You don’t have to present your end-of-semester/ klauzura work for winter semester in December 2025; you will present half of your thesis instead. However, by mid-February 2026, you will have to earn 28 more credits.
